# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive project community.
Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and
other project spaces is expected to be treated with dignity and respect.

## Expected behavior

- Engage honestly, kindly, and with technical focus.
- Respect different viewpoints, backgrounds, and levels of experience.
- Give and receive constructive feedback without personal attacks.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.
- Protect private information and respect clearly expressed boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption,
personal or character attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private
information without permission are not acceptable. Bad-faith use of project
processes to target or silence another participant is also prohibited.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents
the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when
necessary. Responses will be proportionate to the impact, severity, and pattern
of the behavior, and may skip intermediate steps for serious incidents.

## Reporting

Report a possible violation privately using contact information published by
the [`jiya-mira` organization](https://github.com/jiya-mira) or the
[`@Angleline` maintainer](https://github.com/Angleline). If no private contact
method is available, open a minimal issue mentioning `@Angleline` and request a
private follow-up; do not include incident details in that public issue.

Include relevant links or records without unnecessarily exposing personal
information. Maintainers will limit report details to the people needed to
review and address the matter.
